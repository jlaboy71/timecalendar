# Requesting a WFH Day Swap

Follow these steps to request a WFH day swap with a teammate.

## Step 1: Access the WFH Swap Page

From the Dashboard, look for the **WFH Day Swap** button in the My Time Off section. Click it to open the swap page.

Alternatively, you may see a "WFH Day Swap" tile on your dashboard if you have WFH swap access.

## Step 2: Browse Available Days

The swap page displays five day columns - Monday through Friday. Each column shows:
- The **day name** and a colored header
- **Employee names** listed below who have that day as their WFH day
- **Work schedules** (start and end times) if available

### Understanding the Indicators

- **Colored dot**: The employee is available to swap
- **Swap icon**: The employee already has a swap this week (matching colors indicate swap partners)
- **Your entry**: Appears dimmed/disabled - you can't swap with yourself

## Step 3: Select a Teammate

Click on any available teammate's name to open the swap request dialog.

**Note**: You cannot click on:
- Yourself
- Teammates who already have a swap this week

## Step 4: Complete the Request

In the swap dialog:
1. Select the **swap date** from the available options (next occurrences of their WFH day)
2. Write a **message** explaining why you need to swap (required)
3. Click **Send Request**

## Step 5: Wait for Response

Your teammate will:
- Receive an **email notification** immediately
- See your request in their **Incoming Swap Requests** section
- Have **3 business days** to respond before the request expires

## Tracking Your Request

Your sent requests appear in the **Swap History** section at the bottom of the page. You can see:
- The status (Pending, Accepted, Declined, etc.)
- The swap date
- Any response message from your teammate

## Cancelling a Request

If you need to cancel a pending request:
1. Find your request in the Swap History
2. Click the **Cancel** button (red X icon)
3. The request will be cancelled immediately

**Note**: You can only cancel requests that are still pending.
