# Responding to WFH Swap Requests

When a teammate requests to swap WFH days with you, here's how to respond.

## Receiving Requests

When someone requests a swap with you:
1. You'll receive an **email notification** immediately
2. The request appears in your **Dashboard** under "Incoming WFH Swap Requests" (if any are pending)
3. The request also appears on the **WFH Swap page** in the Incoming Requests section

## Viewing Request Details

Each incoming request shows:
- **Who is requesting** - The teammate's name
- **The swap date** - The specific date they want to swap for
- **Their message** - Why they need to swap
- **Status badge** - Showing "Pending"

## Accepting a Request

To accept a swap request:
1. Review the request details
2. Enter a **response message** in the text field (required)
3. Click the **Accept** button (green checkmark)

Once accepted:
- Both you and your teammate are confirmed for the swap
- Neither of you can request or accept another swap for that week
- The swap appears in both your histories with "Accepted" status

## Declining a Request

To decline a swap request:
1. Review the request details
2. Enter a **response message** explaining why you're declining (required)
3. Click the **Decline** button (red X)

When you decline:
- Your teammate is notified
- The request status changes to "Declined"
- Both of you can now pursue other swap options for that week

## Best Practices

- **Respond quickly** - Requests expire after 3 business days
- **Be courteous** - Include a helpful response message, especially when declining
- **Check your calendar** - Make sure you can actually work from the office on the swap date before accepting
- **Consider the reason** - Your teammate may have an important appointment or need

## What If I Don't Respond?

If you don't respond within **3 business days**, the request will automatically expire:
- The status changes to "Expired"
- Your teammate can then request a swap with someone else
- You'll still be able to participate in a different swap that week
