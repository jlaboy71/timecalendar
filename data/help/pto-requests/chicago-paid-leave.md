# Chicago Paid Leave

Chicago Paid Leave is a special leave type available only to employees working in Chicago, Illinois. This leave type is governed by the Chicago Paid Leave and Paid Sick and Safe Leave Ordinance.

## Who Is Eligible?

You are eligible for Chicago Paid Leave if:
- Your work location is set to **Chicago, IL** in your employee profile
- Your account is active

If you don't see Chicago Paid Leave in your balance dashboard but work in Chicago, contact your administrator.

## How It Works

### Annual Allocation
- Chicago employees receive **40 hours (5 days)** of Chicago Paid Leave per year
- This is in addition to your regular Sick Leave balance

### Balance Tracking
Chicago Paid Leave is tracked separately from regular Sick Leave:
- **Total**: Your annual allocation
- **Used**: Hours already taken
- **Pending**: Hours in pending requests (if applicable)
- **Available**: What you can still use

### Carryover
- Unused Chicago Paid Leave **automatically carries over** to the next year
- Maximum carryover is **16 hours (2 days)** per Chicago ordinance
- No action needed - carryover happens during year-end processing

## Using Chicago Paid Leave

### When to Use It
Chicago Paid Leave can be used for:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical appointments
- Caring for a sick family member
- Safety-related absences
- Other reasons covered by the ordinance

### How to Request
1. Go to **Request Time Off**
2. Select **Chicago Paid Leave** as the leave type
3. Choose your date(s)
4. Add any notes (optional)
5. Click **Submit Request**

### Important: No Overdraft Allowed
Chicago Paid Leave has a **hard cap** - you cannot request more hours than you have available. The system will block submission if your request exceeds your balance.

This is different from Vacation, which allows overdraft requests for manager discretion.

## Balance Display

Your Chicago Paid Leave balance appears:
- On your **Dashboard** in the balance cards (if you're a Chicago employee)
- In the **Request Form** when you select Chicago Paid Leave
- In your **Balance Summary** reports

## Questions?

If you have questions about your Chicago Paid Leave balance or eligibility:
1. Check your employee profile for your work location
2. Review your balance history in Reports
3. Contact HR or your administrator for adjustments
